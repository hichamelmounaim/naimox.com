1. Data Collection

We respect your privacy as developers. Our web tools (like the Password Generator and String Manipulator) operate entirely client-side. We do not transmit, log, or store the data you enter into these tools on our servers.

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google uses cookies to track user behavior across the web to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use minimal cookies necessary for site functionality and basic analytics to understand which tutorials are most useful. We do not sell this data to third parties.
